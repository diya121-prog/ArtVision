Project Presentation & Viva Defense Guide

Key talking points, architectural justifications, and answers to anticipated examiner questions.

Executive Summary for Examiners

ArtVision solves the problem of fragmented artwork analysis by introducing a single unified multi-task architecture capable of predicting style, historical movement, artist attribution, genre, mood, and AI authenticity, accompanied by perceptual color tone analysis, visual explainability, and similarity search.

Single Backbone: EfficientNet-B0 Zero-Loss Masking for Sparsity Kendall Uncertainty Weighting Sub-60ms Color Extraction

Anticipated Viva Questions & Model Answers

Q1: Why use Kendall et al. Uncertainty Loss Weighting over equal loss weighting?
Multi-task learning suffers from gradient dominance when task losses operate at heterogeneous scales (e.g. 27-class cross-entropy vs binary BCE). Kendall uncertainty weighting parameterizes homoscedastic task variance ($\sigma_t$), dynamically attenuating noisy/unstable losses and penalizing high variance. Our ablation study proved this increases Style accuracy by +2.5% and macro F1 by +2.7% over equal weighting.
Q2: How does the model handle missing labels across different tasks?
Not every artwork has all annotations (e.g., ArtBench AI images lack artist and genre labels; mood annotations exist on only 1.7% of artworks). We implement loss masking: loss computation for any head is masked to zero when ground truth is null. This allows joint multi-dataset training without discarding incomplete records.
Q3: Why perform color clustering in CIELAB rather than RGB space?
RGB Euclidean distance does not correlate with human perceptual color differences. CIELAB is designed to be perceptually uniform, meaning equal Euclidean distance matches human perception. Furthermore, we implement duplicate swatch merging using $\Delta E_{76} < 12.0$ to prevent gradients from fragmenting into duplicate swatches.
Q4: What is Temperature Scaling and why was it necessary?
Modern deep neural networks are well-known to be overconfident in their Softmax probabilities. Temperature scaling divides logits by a learned scalar $T > 1$ fit on validation NLL without changing argmax predictions. This reduced Expected Calibration Error (ECE) across heads (e.g., from 14.0% to 6.9% on Style), ensuring the reported confidence scores reflect true empirical probabilities.
Q5: How does the model perform on AI-Generated Art Detection?
The authenticity head achieves 97.06% accuracy and an exceptional 0.995 AUROC on the test set. Grad-CAM visual heatmaps confirm that the model focuses on synthetic micro-texture artifacts and structural anomalies introduced by latent diffusion generators.
